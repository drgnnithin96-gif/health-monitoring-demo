Healthy Schools tracker

A working demo by Dr Nithin G N

This is a public demo. Every school, name, number and photo in it is made up. Anything you enter stays in your own browser and is not sent anywhere.

What it is for

Arogya World's Healthy Schools Program teaches diabetes prevention to students in Classes 6 and 7 through 10 activities run over two years. Field coordinators from partner NGOs run the activities in government schools, with a gap of at least 15 days between one activity and the next.

Until this year, coordinators reported each session by posting a photo in a WhatsApp group with the school name, activity number and number of students. We had no way of knowing whether a photo had been sent before, whether the coordinator was actually at the school, or whether the counts were right. A daily Google Form helped a little, but someone still had to go through it by hand every day.

I built this tracker to replace that. The coordinator logs the session on a phone, the app runs the basic checks, and a manager decides whether the session counts.

What the app checks

None of these checks reject a session on their own. They add a note so the manager knows what to look at.

CheckWhy
Logged more than 150 m from the schoolThe coordinator may not have been at the school.
Session shorter than 30 minutesAn activity cannot be done properly in less.
Photo matches one already sentCatches an old photo being sent again, even after resizing.
More students than are enrolled in the classCatches inflated counts.
Less than 15 days since the class's last activityKeeps to the programme's spacing rule.

How a session moves through it

  1. The coordinator picks the school, class and activity number, enters how many students were present and takes a photo. The app records the time and location.
  2. If a session could not be held (exams, a holiday, school closed), the coordinator logs that with a reason instead of leaving a gap.
  3. The session waits in the Review list. A manager at Arogya World or the partner NGO looks at it, with any notes from the checks, and marks it as counted or not counted.
  4. Arogya World managers can flag a session back to the partner with a comment. The partner manager sees the flag and replies.
  5. The register fills in class by class, so it is easy to see which schools are falling behind on the 10 activities.

About the build

Plain HTML and JavaScript, no framework. The field app works on any phone browser and can be added to the home screen. In the live version, data goes to a small online database with separate logins for coordinators, partner managers and Arogya World staff; coordinators cannot see the dashboard. This demo keeps everything in the browser instead.
